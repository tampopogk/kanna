Commit graph: the repo's history as a tab beside the agent

kanna open log [REV...] opens a log pane: commits as rows with coloured lanes, merges drawn as curves, each row with its hash, subject, author and age, and its branches and tags. A branch that is a task's is labelled with the task's name and where it is (Desk, Shelf, merged), and how far it is ahead of and behind main. From the planner it shows main and the repo's task branches; from a task, the task's branch and main. Long merged branches fold into their merge, so main reads as a line of merges. It holds still: new commits wait, and the header says so until ⌘R. This mockup runs on the kanna repo's own history (the newest 700 commits of main and its 33 live task branches).
