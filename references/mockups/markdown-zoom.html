Markdown zoom: pinch magnifies the page; ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 change the text size

Pinch is a plain zoom of the page, like a PDF in Preview: the page keeps the wrap, margins and text size it was laid out with and is drawn bigger or smaller; zoomed in it is wider than the pane and scrolls both ways, and nothing moves on release. ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 step the text size: the page lays out again at the pane's width over the text size, so the text re-wraps to the pane, and the line at the top (tinted) stays at the top. The overview's box follows both.
