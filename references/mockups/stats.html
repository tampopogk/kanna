Stats: a project's numbers over the last 30 days

A third fixed tab in the Desk view, after Desk and Machines. Each number is today's value over a small chart of the last 30 days; hover a chart for a day's value. Quiz: the share of kept quiz answers you got right in the 30 days before each day, by kind and by area, and the misses still to come back. Main: commits and merges (tasks landed) per day. Tasks: how many are open at the end of each day (not done, Desk and Shelf together) with each day's made and done under it, made, started and ended per day, and how long each task took from made to done. Agents: how long task agents worked, how long they waited on you and how soon you answered, how long finished work sat unlooked at, and turns per task with the median turn. Code quality is a placeholder for the next task.
