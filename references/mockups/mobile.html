Kanna on the phone: one repo at a time, the planner in your pocket

Every screen of the native iOS app, in a phone frame at real size (390 × 844 points), for review with markup. Fixtures, not data. The app is one repo at a time; Machines, Stats and the Shelf are all of that repo, and the switcher sits on the repo's name at the top of every tab. Five tabs: Chat, which is the point of the app, then Shelf, Quiz, Stats, Machines. No push notifications in the first version, so nothing here arrives by itself: the app shows what's new when you open it. D3 tags point to the open decisions at the end of the page.
