The Desk view's note stays in sight

The Desk is full and the Shelf is long. Click a Shelf row (or raise a toast's click): the note says why nothing moved. Today it is the last line of the list, under every Shelf row, so with a long Shelf it lands off screen. Here it is a strip along the bottom edge of the Desk view, outside the scrolled list: it shows wherever the list is scrolled, and goes with the next thing you do there, or its ×.
