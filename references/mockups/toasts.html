Toasts: a notice the daemon raises in the front window

A toast stacks below the title bar on the right, newest on top, and fades after 6 seconds (8 with a pane or task), unless the pointer is on it. Clicking one about a pane or task shows that task on the Desk and focuses the pane; × closes it. The daemon raises a keyed toast once: kanna toast "Messages wait for Lint fixes" "aider can't take messages" --pane term-12 --key m:term-12:aider raised twice shows one.
