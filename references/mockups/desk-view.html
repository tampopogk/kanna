Desk view: the planner, the Desk's three tasks, the Shelf below

The Desk holds up to three of a project's tasks, one of them active. The Desk view shows the planning agent on the left and, on the right, the Desk's tasks and, below them, the Shelf: the tasks Kanna has brought in, waiting and run a few at a time in the background. Make a Desk task active (click it, ⌘1 … ⌘3, ⇧⌘A) to see it as it was: its agent docked, its tabs, and the strip of the Desk's other tasks below. ⌘. toggles between the Desk view and the active task, which stays active (and running) while the Desk view shows. Move a Shelf task to the Desk from its row; Task › Move to Shelf moves the active task back.

⌘. the Desk view ⇄ the active task ⌘1 ⌘2 ⌘3 make the Desk's 1st, 2nd, 3rd other task active ⇧⌘A the Desk task waiting longest for you ⌘F search the Shelf (Desk view): ↑ ↓ pick, ↩ Move to Desk, esc clear
