Tab drag

Drag any tab. It follows the cursor and the other tabs slide aside; it settles into its gap with a short spring. Pulled out of the bar it becomes a preview of its window; dropped anywhere off a tab bar, even over another window, the new window grows from it; over any tab bar it folds back into a tab. Escape, or dropping where it started, slides it home. Spec: docs/specs/tab-drag.md.
