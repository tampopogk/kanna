Desk: one task per window, the rest on the Shelf

A task is what a Desk holds: its agent, docked on the left, and its tabs. The Desk shows one; the Shelf below holds the project's other tasks, their agents still running. With no task on it, the Desk shows its planning terminal on the left, beside the full Shelf. A new Desk's planning terminal runs Kanna's planner script: it asks which agent plans (and offers to make that the project's), then starts it with You are the Kanna planning agent.; quit it (/exit) and it's your shell, where you cd elsewhere (the Shelf follows) or start an agent yourself. Tell the planning agent what you want ("split the login work into three tasks") and it adds tasks to the backlog. Tasks are made only by an agent or by hand with kanna task new "NAME" "FIRST MESSAGE" (type it in an agent's pane), then started from the backlog. ⌘N (File › New Desk) opens another Desk window, its planning terminal in ~, asking where: Return takes your latest project, a number another recent one. File › Open Recent opens a recent project's Desk straight away, or brings it forward if it's open. Click a Shelf item to swap, Park to clear the Desk, drag the line between the agent and the tabs, or type a kanna command in the agent's pane as the agent would.
