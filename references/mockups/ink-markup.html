Ink markup: draw on a doc, and the agent beside it works out what the marks mean

Turn on Ink (or hold ⌥ and drag) and draw: circle words, underline them, strike them out, zigzag over them or cross them out with an X, draw an arrow from a paragraph to where it should go, write in the margin. Each stroke is recognised and numbered, and listed in the New ink card: click its kind to correct it, × to remove it. ⌘Z undoes. Stop drawing for 2s and the batch is sent as one thread; let go of ⌥ and it's sent at once. Type straight after a mark to put a note on it (click a mark in the card to note another); ↵ sends. Esc ends a note, or holds the batch to send it yourself.

Below the window: what Send types into the agent, and the marked-up picture it points to. The agent answers mark by mark; marks it acted on leave the page.
