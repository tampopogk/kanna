Serial monitor: a pane on a serial port that the daemon owns

The daemon holds the port, so this pane, kanna serial read and an agent share one connection and one log, kept while the app restarts. Lines that look like ESP-IDF, Zephyr or Arduino logs take their level's colour; ANSI colours show; bytes that aren't text show as escapes, or the whole log as hex. The device reconnects by itself when it's plugged back in. Type in the send line and press Return; ↑ walks its history.
