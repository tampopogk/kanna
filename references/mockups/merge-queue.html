Merge queue: “merge” hands the branch over, and main moves only to what was tested

You tell a ready task's agent to merge. It runs kanna task merge: its agent quits, its tabs close, and the task leaves the Desk for the repo's merge queue, shown on the window's Merge queue tab, beside Desk, Machines, Stats and Schedules: the entries in queue order, then the ones that landed or failed lately. Click an entry and the tab shows its terminal below, the tester at work; click one that ended and it shows the tester's answer and the tests' log. The Desk tab has no merging group. The queue runs one entry at a time: the branch merged onto main as it is now, in a worker, the repo's tests run there, then main fast-forwards to that merge, and the next entry is merged on top of it. A merged entry leaves the queue and its task is Done. One whose tests fail goes back to the Shelf, red, and its agent gets the log. An agent, the queue's tester, runs the tests and decides whether what fails is the branch's doing: a test that fails on main too, or passes when run again alone, doesn't keep the entry out, and one the branch breaks sends it back with the tester's reasons. Every entry is tested in the repo's one queue worktree, whose build stays warm.
